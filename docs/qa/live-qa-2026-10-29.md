## Findings to address

### Secondary ranking includes unverified funding offers

Tech and Sun's secondary list placed Volta Capital fourth at 65/100. Its
[official site](https://volta.capital/) describes Mexican energy investment and
services; an applicable NGO grant offer has not been verified. The record is not
in the lead view, but its placement can waste research time.

Place overview-only records without a verified funding offer behind verified
programs, in a separate research/resource tier. Preserve their records and
uncertainty rather than inventing grant terms or geographic exclusions.

### Applicant and beneficiary rules need structured checks

Verizon appeared third at 65/100 among Tech and Sun's secondary suggestions.
Its [official requirements](https://www.verizon.com/about/responsibility/grant-requirements)
require an eligible IRS public charity or eligible NCES-registered school;
education grants target K–12. NGO status and work with university students do
not establish those conditions. Invitation status alone does not communicate
the main suitability questions.

Add explicit applicant and program-specific beneficiary checks. Keep unconfirmed
facts unknown; Nigeria work alone is not a documented country exclusion.

### Failed prerequisite wording obscures the separate scores

With a failed prerequisite, ERA's MIT Solve details displayed 85/100 thematic fit
alongside "Known prerequisite conflict: score held at zero." The zero refers to
research priority. Name that metric explicitly: "Research priority is 0; thematic
fit is unchanged."

### Actionable coverage remains thin

Three profiles had one lead each; Tech and Sun had none with grant/prize
preferences. New research should prioritize verified California culture,
open-source public goods, Nigeria community energy/connectivity and ecological
restoration calls with practical application routes.

## Scope and evidence

The [structured QA record](live-qa-2026-10-29.json) contains the results, official
source spot checks, publication hashes and limitations. Private project inputs,
browser state, raw logs and local machine paths are excluded.

This is a bounded four-profile review, not a measured directory-wide accuracy
estimate. Applicant facts and unscreened program rules still require review.
Desktop Brave was verified. A temporary 390px viewport request did not change
the actual page width, so this pass supplies no mobile proof. No complete network
trace was captured; the published CSP restricts inference connections to the
same origin.

This report records findings and validation; it does not change matching behavior
or implement the proposed fixes.
