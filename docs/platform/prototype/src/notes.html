A smaller download: what to ship, and when

The review rejected 570 MB on a phone. Research on 4 November 2026 (file sizes from the model listings on Hugging Face, quality figures as the model makers report them) points to a default about half that size, with the larger model as an upgrade. The prototype's prompt now uses these sizes. They need the technical spec owner's check against the spec's golden fixtures before they are a decision.

DeviceWhat runsDownloadWhat it handles
Every deviceKeyword rules and the embeddings the spec already shipsNothing extraThe keyword draft: place, stage, kinds of support
Phone, speechWhisper tiny, multilingual (Moonshine tiny is 28 MB but English only; Whisper base is 77 MB if tiny proves too weak)41 MBDictation in Portuguese, Spanish and English
Phone, assistant, asked firstLFM2.5-350M at 4 bits, or Gemma 3 270M (273 MB plus a 20 MB tokenizer)255 MBShort structured extraction: name, place, stage, needs, offers
Phone, optional upgradeQwen3-0.6B at 4 bits (today's plan)570 MBFuller extraction, for people who want it
ComputerThe browser's own model where it exists (Chrome desktop 148 and later; Edge in preview), otherwise the same models on WebGPU. Speech: Chrome desktop's on-device recognition (139 and later), otherwise Whisper baseNothing for Weave, otherwise 255 or 570 MB; speech nothing or 77 MBAs above

How it gets smaller

  • Start with what costs nothing. The keyword draft works before anything downloads, so nobody waits.
  • Ask after the first draft, with the exact size for this device, framed as what it buys: "Keep your words on this device". Never download without a tap.
  • Default to the 255 MB model on phones. Offer 570 MB as an upgrade in Settings.
  • On a computer, use the browser's own model and speech first, so Weave downloads nothing.
  • Fine-tune one 270 to 350M model to Weave's own schema, and ship it only if it passes the spec's 95 percent schema test. The listings stop at 4 bits; there are no 2 or 3 bit builds to go lower.
  • Resume after a dropped connection, keep the file once, and let people remove it in Settings.

Caveats

  • Quality: no independent extraction benchmark was found. LFM2.5-350M reports 77 on instruction following (IFEval) against 51 for Gemma 3 270M; both are the makers' figures. Test on the golden fixtures.
  • Licence: the LFM models use Liquid's own licence (lfm1.0). Read it before choosing.
  • Chrome's built-in model is desktop only and needs 22 GB free disk, a capable graphics card or 16 GB of memory, and an unmetered first download. Safari has no built-in model, and WebKit opposes the API, so phones get none today.
  • The technical spec calls SpeechRecognition.available() without the langs option, which is required.
  • Not verified: Cache API and OPFS limits, navigator.storage.persist, Background Fetch, resumable range requests, detecting Wi-Fi or Save-Data, and whether a cached model can be shared across sites.

Sources

  • Model file listings: huggingface.co/onnx-community (each model's onnx folder)
  • LFM2.5-350M, LFM2-350M-Extract, Gemma 3 270M, Introducing Gemma 3 270M
  • Chrome Prompt API, LanguageModel browser support, Edge Prompt API, WebKit position
  • SpeechRecognition.available() on MDN

Design rules from both reviews, for people and agents

Written to drop into the repository once placed; each names where it belongs. They extend DESIGN.md and the design skill and change three existing rules, marked.

  1. Use the width. Lists of partners, funding and initiatives are card grids on wide screens. DESIGN.md (Layout), COMPONENTS.md (Card grid), the design skill.
  2. One left edge. Every page lines up with the logo; the home hero and empty-state pages are the centred exceptions. DESIGN.md, templates.css.
  3. Separate pages, not tabs, for Partners and Funding. COMPONENTS.md, the PRD's Matches section.
  4. One filter bar everywhere. Search, filter menus, sort; chosen filters as removable chips with a count. COMPONENTS.md (Filter bar, replacing Directory filters).
  5. No decorative labels above headings. No small-capitals kickers or eyebrows for decoration; a heading and one line. Changes the Eyebrow pattern's use. DESIGN.md, VOICE.md.
  6. Say less, show more. One line under a heading; details behind a link or in a pop-up. Initiatives carry photographs; links show previews. COMPONENTS.md (Media, Link preview).
  7. The kinds of support by their own names. Living, Material, Intellectual, Experiential, Social, Financial, Cultural, Spiritual as titles, the plain meaning below. Changes VOICE.md's plain-name table for these eight. VOICE.md, DL-014.
  8. Explain compact signs with tooltips. Tiles, kind tags, types of work and fit lines open a native tooltip; the same words stay available to screen readers. COMPONENTS.md (Tooltip).
  9. Align what people compare. On cards in a row, the line people scan (fit) sits at the same height. COMPONENTS.md (Card).
  10. Describing is a conversation led by voice. One short question at a time; Speak first, typing for when speaking is not possible, files and links at any point; words can be changed after; the on-device model builds the card from everything at the end. Needs and offers the same, with their cards beside. PRD 6.1, COMPONENTS.md (Conversation).
  11. At most five needs and five offers, each with every kind it carries. COMPONENTS.md (Ask card), PRD.
  12. Partners are for initiatives on the list. Private initiatives see funding only; partner names, cards and introductions open when the initiative is shared with the community. PRD (R16), 08.
  13. Two ways to be on Weave. Private, for finding funding, or shared with the community. 08.
  14. One email door. Anything that needs an email saves the initiative to it; signed in, actions just happen. 08.
  15. Save where the reason is. The email field sits in the card that shows what saving or sharing opens. 08, DL-018.
  16. Small tasks and information open over the page. Sign in, save, sharing, delete, What we store and the assistant record are pop-ups: a dialog on wide screens, a sheet on phones. Changes the Code panel's "never a modal" and the Dialog's "never for saving". COMPONENTS.md.
  17. Settings are set in place. Choices change where they are shown; leaving (sign out, delete) sits at the bottom, set apart. COMPONENTS.md (Settings template).
  18. A footer on every page. The same links and the content line. COMPONENTS.md (Footer).
  19. Equal heights. A field and its button share one height; steps of one flow keep one height. COMPONENTS.md, review checklist.
  20. Motion says what changed; the backdrop canvas is the one decoration, behind Home only, and stops under reduced motion. DESIGN.md (Motion, Elevation).
  21. Downloads are optional and sold as privacy. Size for this device, nothing without a tap, resumable, removable. Technical spec.
  22. Phone first, without jumps. New screens start at the top; the hero stays short on phones. Review checklist.

Checklist additions: any list in one narrow column at 1280 px; any tab where a page would do; any list without search, a filter and a sort; any decorative label above a heading; any fit or price line at different heights across a row; any compact sign without a tooltip; any small task or information page that leaves the page; any partner name shown to a private initiative.

How it is built

  • The design system's css/, tokens/ and fonts/ are published unchanged; only css/index.css is linked. The icon sprite is inlined unchanged; two proposed icons sit in a separate sprite.
  • prototype.css holds the chrome (proto-) and the proposed patterns (rcp-), using tokens. Three scripts hold the fictional content, the screens and the behaviour. Nothing is sent or stored.
  • The 360 px frame pins viewport rules to their phone values; sheets, menus and tooltips are placed over the frame so it behaves like a phone.

Using the controls

  • Screen picks one of 22 screens; State lists that screen's states. Sheet and pop-up screens draw the page underneath and open the sheet in the chosen state; closing it returns to the page.
  • Theme follows the system or pins light or dark. Width draws the 360 px frame or fills the window. Reset walkthrough forgets the session.

Usability round before 30 November

Half a day, three participants, same-day debrief, following packages/design-system/principles/usability-testing.md. Run on this prototype, on the participant's own phone where possible.

Participants

  • P1: runs a place-based group, phone only, weak connection welcome; Portuguese or Spanish speaker welcome.
  • P2: a researcher or tool builder who looks for co-applicants, on a laptop.
  • P3: new to Regen Commons, on a phone.

Before the session

  • Open the link, press Reset walkthrough, set Width to Phone 360 on a laptop, leave Theme on System, hide the Notes.
  • Say once: the screens use an invented example, and the assistant does not hear or read their words yet; it plays the example back.

Script

  1. You are helping us check an app before it exists. You are not being assessed, and no move is a mistake.
  2. Say what you notice, expect and wonder. I stay quiet and answer at the end. If stuck, carry on as you would alone.
  3. You can stop at any time. With permission I record screen and voice for the team only, and delete it after review.
  4. Warm up: a project you care about, and how you look for partners or funding today.
  5. Task cards one at a time. If they go quiet, ask what is going through their mind.
  6. At the end: what confused you, what did you expect, what would you tell a friend this is?
