# Business model canvas: Regen Commons Weave (working name)

Status: draft for council review · Date: 2026-10-31
Companion documents: [proposal brief](01-proposal-brief.md) · [PRD](04-prd.md) · [research digest](06-research-digest.md)

This is a commons, so the canvas has the nine standard blocks plus three that matter for a shared resource: governance, the value returned to the commons, and what is deliberately left out. Figures are planning estimates for the first eighteen months.

## 1. Customer segments

| Segment | Size signal | What they come for |
|---|---|---|
| Place-based initiatives (community hubs, restoration crews, agroecology, water, youth) in the network's regions: Nigeria, Brazil, California, Costa Rica, Spain, Kenya and beyond | Regen Coordination round applicants, Bloom chapters, Greenpill chapters, ReFi local nodes; the hub doc holds seven rows after one month of manual effort | Funding fit, partners who complete them, visibility of their non-financial capital |
| Technology and tooling builders (Green Goods, Gardens, Coop, Bread Coop, GrowFi, City/Sync) | Dozens inside the networks | Pilot communities and co-applicants for ecosystem grants |
| Research and methodology groups (Metagov and KOI researchers, universities, MRV and valuation researchers) | A handful of active partners today, growing with joint proposals | Place-based partners, funders that want a research component |
| Artists, storytellers and culture bearers | Under-served by every funder directory | A place where cultural and spiritual capital counts |
| Funders and allocators (program leads, Gitcoin and Giveth round operators, bioregional financing facilities, foundations such as those in the 699-funder catalog) | The catalog itself is the prospect list | Ready partnerships and a picture of regional needs |
| Network stewards (Regen Commons council, Bloom, Greenpill, ReFi DAO, Regen Coordination, BioFi community) | Six to ten organisations | Shared infrastructure instead of parallel spreadsheets |

## 2. Value propositions

For initiatives: tell your story once, by voice if you like, share it, and get ranked partners and funding opportunities with reasons, every week, for your region. Your needs and offers across eight forms of capital become legible to funders who only saw money before.

For funders: see who is ready to apply together, what a watershed or bioregion needs, and which initiatives already hold the living, cultural and social capital a grant should multiply.

For networks: one open, forkable layer for initiatives, needs, offers and opportunities that each network can curate and cross-post to, with open identifiers and schemas instead of another silo.

For the commons: trusted, consented, place-based data that improves curation every month and feeds the knowledge commons.

Differentiators: local AI by default; eight-capital needs and offers (no comparable directory tags by capital form); partnership scoring, not only fit scoring; bioregion and watershed curation; built on a maintained, evidence-graded catalog of 699 funders and 768 programs with a weekly research cadence.

## 3. Channels

- regencommons.com and the platform subdomain; the weekly pulse email.
- Community Spotlight calls (first demo 5 November 2026), Regen Coordination Hub posts, Telegram funding topics.
- Partner network sessions: Bloom's offers-and-needs working session (17 November 2026), Greenpill and ReFi DAO chapters, BioFi community of practice deep dives, Grassroots Economics social-soil calls.
- Murmurations index and partner directories for discovery outside the network.
- Challenge cohorts (time-boxed "apply together" sprints) and proposal clinics around major deadlines.

## 4. Customer relationships

Self-serve first, with value shown before any email is asked for. An account is not membership. Stewards tidy, they do not filter. Regional calls build recipes with artists and researchers. Challenges create accountability and a measurable outcome (groups formed, proposals submitted). Introductions are relayed with consent, never cold. A funder desk (Phase 2) gives allocators a curated regional view and a way to publish calls.

## 6. Key resources

- The funder catalog: 699 funders, 768 program documents, 1,588 official source URLs, researched eligibility rules and conditions, a Monday discovery and Thursday freshness routine that produces reviewed pull requests.
- The Regen Commons substrate: the Supabase project with Auth, members and stewards; the steward desk; the brand, tokens and fonts, now defined as a shared [design system](../../DESIGN.md); the constitution; the member organisations.
- The local AI stack already in production (embeddings and ranking) and the proven patterns from Coop (tiering, repair loops, review gates, on-device Whisper).
- Relationships: Metagov and KOI, Grassroots Economics and Cosmo-Local Credit, Bloom, Greenpill, ReFi DAO, Regen Coordination, BioFi, Fito Network (the original 593-record snapshot), the Regen Knowledge Commons group.
- A lead builder with agentic tooling, and council members willing to steward regions.

## 7. Key activities

Catalog curation (weekly), platform development by phase, steward verification and regional curation, Community Spotlight demos and Hub posts, challenge cohorts around deadlines, joint proposals (Mozilla, NLnet, rounds), consent and privacy policy upkeep, open releases of data and schemas, evaluation of matching quality and local AI tiers on real devices.

## 10. Governance

Housed under Regen Commons as a resource open to all networks; the council are the administrators through the steward desk; access tier, data policy and sharing targets are council decisions recorded in the repository; partner networks participate as member organisations; code and data are open so any network can fork. Accountability runs two ways: to the communities represented (consent, retraction, no scraping) and to funders (evidence-graded catalog, explained matches, measurable outcomes).

## 11. Value returned to the commons

Every shared record strengthens the commons: a trusted directory, regional needs maps, recipes that worked, and structured sources for the knowledge commons. The platform makes Regen Commons membership tangible (a utility, not only a constitution) and gives the network a coherent story for funders: place-based communities plus research plus tooling plus culture, coordinated around capital in all its forms.

## 12. Deliberately out of scope

Running funding rounds, holding money, issuing tokens or wallets at launch, scraping other directories, selling data, and acting as a fiscal sponsor. These belong to partners or to later phases under council decision.
