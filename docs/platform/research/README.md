# Research reports

Working reports produced on 2026-10-31 for the platform plan. Each carries inline citations and a Sources list; facts were checked on that date and should be rechecked before relying on them later. Statements marked as inference are the researcher's reading, not a source statement.

- [Ecosystem, forms of capital and commitment pooling](ecosystem-capitals-pooling.md): the Eight Forms of Capital, the Offers and Needs Market, Hylo, Grassroots Economics and Cosmo-Local Credit, Murmurations and adjacent platforms, name collisions.
- [KOI, bioregion and watershed data, funding](koi-bioregions-funding.md): the KOI-net protocol and packages, dataset licences and lookup services, grant programmes and fiscal hosting.
- [Local AI and platform stack](local-ai-and-stack.md): in-browser inference options, speech and document extraction, consented cloud fallback, Supabase, Resend, Vercel, matching references.

`local-only/` is ignored by git. It holds surveys of the Regen Commons website and of Coop and the Knowledge Commons repositories, and an extract of the council sync transcript. They are unreviewed working notes on internal discussion, so they stay out of the public repository; share them privately.
