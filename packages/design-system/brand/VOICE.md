# Voice

This guide owns the words in Regen Commons products. It applies to labels, messages, errors, emails and the assistant.

## Voice

The voice stays the same everywhere.

- **Plain first.** Use the word a newcomer would use. Explain framework terms in help, not in labels.
- **Warm and direct.** Write to one person, as "you". Use "we" only when Regen Commons actually does something.
- **Honest.** Say what is uncertain, what is stored, who can see it and what happens next.
- **Specific.** Name the place, the fund, the date and the amount. Avoid general claims about impact.
- **Brief.** Cut half the words, then look again. A prompt is one sentence. A hint is one or two.

## Tone map

Tone shifts with the situation. Botanical language appears only in the top rows, where nothing depends on it.

| Situation | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Website, hero, about | Warm, a little poetic | The ground beneath the work. |
| Empty state | Inviting, specific | No partners here yet. When one fits what you need, it appears here. |
| Conversation | Curious, brief | Where does most of your work happen? |
| Suggestion from the assistant | Tentative, checkable | I found three needs. Keep what is right and change what is not. |
| Success | Plain, a little warm | Saved. Only you can see it. |
| Asking for an email | Plain about the benefit | Email yourself a code to save it and open it on any device. |
| Error | Exact, calm, with a next step | We could not send your code. Your work is still here. Try again in a minute. |

| Stale information | Plain | May be out of date. Last confirmed in March. |
| Consent and privacy | Exact, complete, no metaphor | Only the text you just wrote is sent. It is not kept after the reply. |
| Money and eligibility | Exact, no metaphor | Check eligibility with the funder before you apply. |
| Steward desk | Neutral and precise | Hidden by a steward on 12 October. Reason: duplicate. |

## Saving and signing in

Use one door and one family of verbs: Save and Sign in. Account and membership are different things, so keep the words apart.

| Moment | Words |
|---|---|
| The offer | Keep this initiative. Email yourself a code to save it and open it on any device. Only you can see it until you choose to share it. |
| Buttons | Email me a code. Not now. |
| Field hint | No password. We only use this for your code and anything you turn on later. |
| After sending | We sent a code to ana@example.org. Wrong email? Send a new code. |
| Waiting | It can take a minute to arrive. Check your spam folder if it does not. |
| Success | Saved. Only you can see it. |
| Before saving | Saved on this device only. |
| Gated action | Ask for an introduction. They need a way to reply, so this asks for your email. |

## Words we avoid

- **Account language:** create an account, sign up, register, log in, join (for saving), member (before membership is the actual topic).
- **Pressure:** hurry, don't miss out, limited time, last chance, urgent, streak, leaderboard, countdown, unlock, level up.
- **Hype:** revolutionary, seamless, powerful, cutting-edge, game-changing, empower.
- **Vague help:** oops, something went wrong, please try again later, with nothing about what happened.
- **Insider terms in labels:** bioregional realm, capital stack, KOI, RID, ORN, T0 to T4.

## Mechanics

- Sentence case everywhere, including factual record labels.
- A button says what happens: Save my initiative, not Submit. An action keeps its name through the flow.
- No arrows in links or buttons. No em dashes in product copy.
- No decorative kickers or eyebrows above headings. Keep a factual label only where no heading can carry its meaning, in sentence case (DL-028).
- Write dates in full: 30 November 2026. Write money with the currency: R$ 60,000.
- Merge labels into values: "3 needs, 2 offers", not "Needs: 3".
- Leave room for Portuguese and Spanish, which often run a third longer than English. Never build a sentence from fragments.
- Accept what people type. Trim spaces, ignore case, keep what they entered when something fails.
