# Regen Commons brand

This guide defines the brand across the website, the steward desk, products and email. [DESIGN.md](../../../DESIGN.md) owns exact values, and [VOICE.md](VOICE.md) owns language. Everything here describes what regencommons.com already does, except where marked as a proposal.

## What it is

Regen Commons is a home for shared regenerative work. It holds the ground beneath a network of people, places and projects, so they can find each other and work together. Its values are care, stewardship, transparency, reciprocity, plurality, collaboration and integrity.

The brand should feel like a community's field notebook. It is warm and handmade, closely observed, useful in the field, and shared.

## Character

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Warm and grounded | Slick or corporate |
| Plain about tasks | Clever at the cost of clarity |
| Patient and inviting | Urgent or pushy |
| Specific about places and people | Abstract about impact |
| A commons people tend together | A platform that owns its users |

## Language of images

Five families of metaphor run through the website. Use them where nothing depends on being understood: titles, empty states, the website.

- **Botanical ground:** seeds, roots, soil, seasons.
- **Mycelium network:** hidden connection, branching, exchange.
- **Commoning as a verb:** tending, holding, sharing.
- **Stewardship:** care over time, not ownership.
- **Field notebook:** observation, specimens, pencil and ink.

## The mark

The mark is a mycelium network growing from one point: branching lines around a solid core.

- [mark.svg](logo/mark.svg) is the static mark and takes its colour from the surrounding text. Use it in products and documents.
- [favicon.svg](logo/favicon.svg) is the mark in a dark circle, for browser tabs and app icons.
- The living mark is a small canvas on the website, `logo-canvas.js`. The website owns it. It stays still under reduced motion. Products use the static mark.

Rules:

- Keep clear space of at least half the mark's width on all sides.
- The smallest size is 24 px.
- Use one colour. Don't add effects, outlines or new colours, and don't stretch it.
- Set the name beside the mark in Fraunces. The website's tracked capital label stays on the website until it adopts the 12 px floor.

## Names

- **Regen Commons** is the organisation and the commons. Write it in two words with both capitals.
- **Product names** sit beside the mark, top-left, and return to that product's home. A short word such as Weave cannot explain itself, so it always appears near a plain tagline on first contact.
- **Weave** is a working name that the council has not yet confirmed. See the [open decisions](../../../docs/platform/open-decisions.md). Keep it out of component names and tokens, so a rename touches only copy and configuration.

## Colour story

The palette comes from soil, plants and metal in low light. [DESIGN.md](../../../DESIGN.md#colors) has the values.

| Name | Role in the brand |
|---|---|
| Paper, Parchment, Vellum | The notebook's surfaces. Dark theme is botanical darkness. Light theme is warm vellum. |
| Ink, Ink Mid, Ink Dim | Writing, from most to least prominent. |
| Forest, Moss | Growth. Forest is the colour of action. |
| Copper | Hairlines and small labels. The brand's quiet signature. |
| Gold, Amber | Warmth and attention. Gold marks keyboard focus. |
| Signal, Teal, Circuit, Sage | Water, instruments and new growth. They are accents and capital hues. |
| Madder | A plant dye red, added for errors and destructive actions. New in this system. |

The website gives each page its own accent: forest for the landing page, copper for learn and join, gold for seeds, teal for the map. Products do not need page accents.

## Type story

- **Fraunces** gives titles an old-style warmth. Light weights appear only at display sizes. The website's italic accent in hero titles stays on the website.
- **Source Serif 4** is for reading: prose, assistant messages, reasons.
- **Space Grotesk** is for tasks and data: controls, labels, tables, hints.

Fonts are self-hosted under the Open Font License. See [fonts/README.md](../fonts/README.md).

## Two devices that are ours

These are the system's memorable ideas. Use them consistently, and resist adding others.

- **Capital tiles.** Each form of capital has a two-letter glyph, like a specimen label or a periodic table. A strip of eight shows at a glance what a group needs and offers. Filled means confirmed, dashed means suggested, and hollow means not held.
- **Pencil and ink.** Anything an assistant suggests is drawn in pencil: dashed, quiet, clearly provisional. When a person confirms it, it becomes ink. This shows the difference between what a person said and what software guessed, and it is true to how a field notebook works.

## Imagery and texture

- Line icons at 24 by 24, stroke 1.5, round caps. See [icons.svg](icons.svg).
- The mycelium canvas and paper grain belong behind one hero region at most, never behind inputs or rows. Both stop moving under reduced motion.
- Use photographs of real people and places, with their permission and with credit. No stock photos. Crop to a fixed ratio with a faint inner outline.

## Motion

Motion answers what a person does: opening, confirming, a tile turning from pencil to ink. Pages do not animate in by themselves. The living canvas on the website is the one exception, and it respects reduced motion.
