# Interface principles

UI work in Regen Commons products follows three books. These notes say what each one means for this product, in our own words, with page references for anyone who wants the source. Page numbers are PDF pages of the editions below.

| Book | What it decides | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Steve Krug, *Don't Make Me Think, Revisited*, third edition, New Riders, 2014 | How people use what we build | [dont-make-me-think.md](dont-make-me-think.md) |
| Adam Wathan and Steve Schoger, *Refactoring UI* | How a screen shows what matters | [refactoring-ui.md](refactoring-ui.md) |
| Brad Frost, *Atomic Design*, 2016 | How the system stays whole | [atomic-design.md](atomic-design.md) |

The PDFs are licensed copies held by the team and are never committed. Buy your own, or ask a maintainer. Do not paste passages from them into this repository.

Two working documents turn the books into habits.

- [ui-review-checklist.md](ui-review-checklist.md) has the checks for any UI change. The [five-minute review](../../../DESIGN.md#how-we-follow-them) is the short version.
- [usability-testing.md](usability-testing.md) is the plan for watching people use the product.

Where the books conflict with each other or with this product, [DESIGN.md](../../../DESIGN.md) and the [decision log](../decision-log.md) record what we chose. All three are guidance about typical situations. When real users show otherwise, believe the users.
